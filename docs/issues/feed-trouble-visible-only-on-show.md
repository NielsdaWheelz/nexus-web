# feed trouble is visible only on its show

the library's source lines and the feed indexes carry no check or addition state.
a failed recurring check or stopped once-only addition therefore has no entrance
from those surfaces. its facts and commands live on the show's title page.

this is the accepted sigla scope (owner decision d15, 2026-11-17), replacing
`LibraryFeedsSection.tsx` at `ef44ba92d`, which showed row attention and additions.
impact: trouble can remain unnoticed unless the affected show is opened.

resolved when an agreed discovery surface leads to unresolved feed trouble
without returning progress or commands to the library's source lines.
