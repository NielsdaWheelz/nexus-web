# today's page is printed under four names

found 2026-11-15 at `42618db32`, photographing the phone palette.

## problem

one page, four spellings on the phone alone:

- the place: `Today` (the chip, and the registry's label).
- its pane's row in the palette: `journal · November 15, 2026`.
- its line in the notes list: `November 15, 2026`.
- the verb that writes to it: `add to today`, and the slip `added to today`.

alongside it, the settings place opens a pane titled `Account`, beside the
palette's own `account` row.

## impact

a reader who typed `today` and landed on a pane called `journal · …` has to
learn that they are one thing. related naming records:
`pane-current-state-has-two-names.md`,
`resource-title-and-unavailable-wording-repeat.md`.

## resolved when

the place, its pane's title and the verb's object agree on one name for the
daily page, or the difference is stated as a rule (the place is `today`, a past
day is its date).
