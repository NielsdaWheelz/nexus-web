# settings panes mix copy case and measure

problem: account and librarian settings use lowercase body copy and a centred
36rem `PaneSurface measured`; appearance, reader and keyboard shortcuts retain
their existing copy case and layout.

impact: moving between settings siblings changes their composition and voice.
this is an accepted boundary of `docs/account-settings-spec.md` decisions d3,
d12; destination names already have one registry owner.

resolved when a separately scoped settings-family design applies one deliberate
copy and measure contract to every sibling, verified at phone and desktop widths.
